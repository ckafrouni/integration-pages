01 / Purpose

A personal layer,
not a public platform.

Depending on the permissions granted, integrations can read, search, and manage authorized email, files, calendars, contacts, documents, spreadsheets, and tasks.

Optional AI-assisted workflows can summarize information or help carry out tasks. Access is limited by the connected account’s granted scopes and the provider’s rules.

This website is static documentation. It has no app login and does not process integration data.
