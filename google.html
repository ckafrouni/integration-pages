Purpose

Personal work,
within granted scopes.

Where permitted, workflows can read, search, and manage authorized email, files, calendars, contacts, documents, spreadsheets, and tasks. Optional AI assistance can summarize information or support account-holder-authorized tasks.

Google OAuth access is limited to the granted scopes. Tokens are stored encrypted in Executor, in an operator-controlled, self-hosted runtime. This does not imply that every other stored record is encrypted or has a verified retention schedule.

Relevant data may be sent to configured agent or model providers during an authorized AI workflow. No provider list or zero-retention or no-training configuration has been verified for this documentation.

Policy restrictions

Google Limited Use.

Use of Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This policy prohibits selling Google user data, using it for advertising, or using it for general-purpose AI or machine-learning model training. These are restrictions on permitted use, not a claim that all configured providers have zero retention or verified no-training settings.

This documentation does not claim Google verification, certification, or endorsement.

Your control

Revoke access.
Request deletion.

The account holder can revoke access in their Google account’s connected-app permissions. Previously persisted histories, logs, or backups are not automatically deleted by revocation.

The operator handles deletion requests, including local stores. See the privacy policy for storage and disclosure details, and contact guidance for private requests.
