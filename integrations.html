Authorization

Only within the grant.

OAuth authorizes access according to the scopes granted by the account holder. A scope may permit reading, searching, creating, updating, or deleting certain resources; the provider determines what each permission allows.

A product listed here does not imply every capability is available or authorized. Optional AI assistance may use relevant authorized data with configured agent or model providers.

Permissions are revocable. Revocation does not automatically erase earlier task histories, logs, or backups. See the privacy policy and deletion request guidance.
